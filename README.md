# About Us

We are the **ARMADILLOS**, a robotics team from **SESI 024 Tatuí, São Paulo, Brazil**.

We design, build and program robots while developing projects that combine **technology, engineering, creativity and teamwork**.

Our journey is built around competitive robotics, where every season brings a new challenge — from designing mechanisms and programming autonomous systems to developing innovative projects and artistic presentations.

We compete in two major robotics formats:

- **FIRST LEGO League (FLL)**
- **OBR Artística — Brazilian Robotics Olympiad**

> **We don't just build robots. We build experiences, solutions and the future.**

# Competitions

## FIRST LEGO League

The **FIRST LEGO League** challenges teams to design, build and program a LEGO robot while developing an innovative project connected to each season's challenge.

ARMADILLOS has participated in multiple FLL seasons since **2010**, returning to competition in 2021 and continuing through the current generation.

### FLL History

- **Body Forward** — 2010–11
- **Food Factor** — 2011–12
- **World Class** — 2014–15
- **Cargo Connect** — 2021–22
- **Super Powered** — 2022–23
- **Master Piece** — 2023–24
- **Submerged** — 2024–25
- **Unearthed** — 2025–26

### Recent Highlights

**Unearthed · 2025–26**

- **1st place — Core Values**
- **1st place — Robot Challenge**
- **2nd place — Robot Performance**

**Submerged · 2024–25**

- **2nd place — Innovation Project**
- **2nd place — Technical Award**

---

## OBR Artística

The **OBR Artística** combines robotics, performance, storytelling and technology.

Our presentations use robots as part of an artistic narrative, transforming engineering projects into complete experiences.

### 2025 — Tempus

A story about **Tempus**, a scientist, and his robot assistant **All-In**, who travel through different eras searching for what made people smile.

**Results**

- **1st place — Regional**
- **3rd place — State**
- **5th place — National**
- **Qualified for the international stage**

### 2026 — Padrinhos Mágicos

Our 2026 presentation creates an analogy between **artificial intelligence and the excessive use of technology**, using the universe of *The Fairly OddParents* to tell a story about responsibility, balance and the consequences of unlimited wishes.

**Results**

- **1st place — Regional**
- **4th place — State**
- **Qualified for the National Stage**
