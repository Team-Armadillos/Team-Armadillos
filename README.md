# About Us

We are the **ARMADILLOS**, a robotics team from **SESI 024 Tatuí, São Paulo, Brazil**.

Our journey combines **robotics, programming, engineering, creativity and teamwork** through competitive robotics.

We compete in two major formats:

<div align="center">

| Competition | Focus |
| :---: | :--- |
| **FIRST LEGO League** | Robotics · Innovation · Teamwork |
| **OBR Artística** | Robotics · Art · Storytelling · Technology |

</div>

We design, build and program robots while developing projects that transform technology into real experiences.

> **We don't just build robots. We build experiences, solutions and the future.**

---

# Robotics Journey

Competitive robotics is at the heart of ARMADILLOS.

Our work brings together **software, electronics, mechanics, automation, 3D design and artistic performance**.

### What we do

- Robot programming and autonomous systems
- Electronics and sensors
- Mechanical design and construction
- 3D modeling and prototyping
- 3D printing
- Automation
- Innovation projects
- Artistic robotics and storytelling
- Teamwork and problem-solving

# OBR Artística

The **OBR Artística** combines robotics, performance, storytelling and technology.

Our presentations transform engineering projects into complete artistic experiences.

## 2025 · TEMPUS

A story about **Tempus**, a scientist, and his robot assistant **All-In**, traveling through different eras searching for what made people smile.

<div align="center">

| Stage | Result |
| :---: | :---: |
| Regional | **1st Place** |
| State | **3rd Place** |
| National | **5th Place** |

</div>

The team also qualified for the **international stage**.

---

## 2026 · PADRINHOS MÁGICOS

Our 2026 presentation explores the relationship between **artificial intelligence, technology and responsible use**, using the universe of *The Fairly OddParents* as the artistic narrative.

<div align="center">

| Stage | Result |
| :---: | :---: |
| Regional | **1st Place** |
| State | **4th Place** |
| National | **Qualified** |

</div>
